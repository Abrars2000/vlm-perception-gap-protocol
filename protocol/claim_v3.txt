CLAIM v3 — VLM ATTRIBUTE-LEVEL PERCEPTION GAP (EXPLORATORY)

Claim
We test whether attribute-level Gap = A − C is predicted by P1 (language prior) and P2 (encoder-feature separability), computed before VLM answer scoring, and whether the prediction transfers to unseen attributes (LOAO) and unseen models (LOMO).

Predictors
- P1 (language prior): primary.
- P2 (encoder-feature separability): primary.
- P3 (projector bandwidth): sensitivity analysis only. P3 is not part of the primary predictive law and is not used to support any primary claim.

Novelty boundary
Gap existence is not claimed as new. Prior work (BlindTest, Theodoridis et al., Chen et al.) already reports probe-versus-VLM-answer gaps. The contribution is attribute-level prediction from pre-answer predictors and held-out transfer to unseen attributes and models.

Status
Exploratory. LOAO and LOMO are generalisation checks, not confirmatory tests.
Success criterion for the generalisation checks is defined in kill_conditions.md (H4): pooled out-of-fold LOAO R² ≥ 0.10 and LOMO R² ≥ 0.10, each with a 95% cluster-bootstrap CI lower bound above 0.

Operational meaning
- VLM checkpoints are frozen, already-pretrained public checkpoints. No task-specific training, fine-tuning or weight updates.
- P1 is computed from a fixed caption corpus; P2 from frozen encoder features and attribute labels. Both are computed before VLM answer scoring.
- Linear probes are diagnostic readouts only. They measure A (encoder) and B (projected tokens) and do not update any VLM weights.
- "Pre-answer" means computed before VLM answer scoring. It does not mean before original VLM pre-training.
